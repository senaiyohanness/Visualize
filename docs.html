Spotify Track Visualization Project

Design Process

I started with a 2,500 songs Spotify dataset that includes danceability, energy, duration, genre, and other track information. My goal was to let users explore how musical traits relate across different genres and years. I began by sketching how filters and axes could be laid out. I chose a scatterplot because it's simple to understand and works well for comparing two variables at a time.

I tested pairings like Danceability vs Energy and added dropdowns so users can change which metrics show on the X and Y axes. The genre legend helps users interpret clusters. Once the layout and interactivity were solid, I deployed the visualization using GitHub Pages.

Design Choices

  • Scatterplot: good for exploring relationships between numeric features like energy and danceability.
  • Color encoding: used to distinguish genres visually and identify groupings.
  • Dropdowns: let users explore different numeric variables (danceability, energy, duration).
  • Tooltips: show the song title, artist, genre, explicit status, and duration on hover.
  • Filters: genre and year filters allow focused exploration of subsets.

Findings and Discoveries

I found that high energy songs tend to also have high danceability, especially in genres like hip hop and EDM. Traditional genres such as folk and world music tend to have lower average energy. The visualization makes it easy to spot genre patterns and outliers.
